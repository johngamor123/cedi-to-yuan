←How It Works
💱

Buy Yuan in 5 easy steps

1
Check today's rate
The Home screen shows how many Yuan you get for 1 Cedi. Use the quick calculator to try any amount.
2
Enter your amount
Tap Buy Yuan, type how many Cedis you want to spend, or pick a quick amount. You see the Yuan you will get.
3
Add where to send your Yuan
Choose Alipay, WeChat Pay or a Chinese bank. Write the full name exactly as it is on that account so your money goes to the right person.
4
Place your order and pay
Pay with Mobile Money or bank transfer using the payment details we show you. Then type your transaction ID and tap "I Have Paid".
5
We confirm and send
We confirm your payment and send your Yuan. You get a notification 🔔 and can follow every step in My Orders.

Questions

Is my money safe?
Every order has its own ID and status that you can follow in My Orders. We only send Yuan after we confirm your payment.
What if I typed the wrong account?
Message us right away on WhatsApp before we send. Always double check the name on your account.
Can I cancel an order?
Yes, as long as you have not paid. If you already paid, contact support.
